In Mission,We are One

The 2026 Annual Catholic Appeal enables us to fund the Diocese of San Diego's ministry offices and programs that help our community members to live their faith. These reinforce our Catholic schools, sustain our retired clergy and those preparing for the priesthood, support faith formation at all ages, strengthen families, promote a culture of life, care for creation, engage youth and young adults and extend a helping hand to the needy.
