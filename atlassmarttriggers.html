Draft for review
Atlas creates a draft in your Action Queue. You review, personalise if needed, then send. Best for major gift thank yous.
How you'll be notified: When this trigger fires, a notification bell alert appears in Atlas, and the draft is added to your Action Queue. You'll also see it on your dashboard. Drafts expire after 7 days if not actioned.
Auto-send guardrails: Atlas validates the template is complete before activating this rule. If a donor has no email address on file when the trigger fires, the email is skipped and a task is created in your Action Queue instead.
