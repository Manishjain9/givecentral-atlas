Tags & Segments

Tags are labels applied to donor records. Atlas Intelligence tags are auto-generated by the system and Super-admin tags are governance-controlled — both are fixed and can't be added or edited here. Staff labels are the ones your team creates and archives — once created, tags and segments can't be edited. Segments are saved filters that resolve against live donor data on demand.
