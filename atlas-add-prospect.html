What the grades mean — click a letter in any card to re-grade
AExcellent — strong, verified, top tier (e.g. board-level linkage · $10k+ capacity · active enthusiasm)
BGood — solid signal, some verification (known relationship · $1k–$10k capacity · past giving)
CModerate — plausible but unproven; needs qualification before investing officer time
DWeak — thin evidence; keep in identification, don't assign an officer yet
FNone — no linkage / capacity / interest found; do not advance
Ceiling rule: the prospect's overall priority is capped by their lowest of the three grades — here B (Linkage). Raise the weakest axis to raise the ceiling.
