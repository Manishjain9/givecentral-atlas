Multi-Factor Authentication

Add an extra layer of security to your admin account at every login.
MFA adds a second step to your login: after your password, you confirm it's really you with a one-time code from your phone. This way, even if someone learns your password, they still can't access your account without that second code.

Set up Google Authenticator

Scan the QR code with your authenticator app, then enter the 6-digit code it shows.
  1. Open Google Authenticator on your phone.
  2. Tap + → Scan a QR code.
  3. Can't scan? Enter this key manually:
JBSW Y3DP EHPK 3PXP
