Account recovery

A reset link is
safer than a password.

Atlas never sends your existing password by email. Instead we send a one-time link that expires in 30 minutes, ties to a single device, and writes an entry into the audit log the moment it's used.

Links expire in 30 minutes. If you don't reset within that window, request a new one — old links stop working.
Single-use. Each reset link works exactly once. Click it on the same device you requested it from.
SSO users: if your diocese signs in with Microsoft 365 or Google, the reset happens with your identity provider instead.

Forgot your password?

Enter the work email on your Atlas account and we'll send a single-use link to reset it. The link expires after 30 minutes.

Diocese on SSO? If your team signs in with Microsoft 365 or Google, this won't work — use SSO instead or ask your diocesan admin to reset.

Trouble signing in? Contact your administrator or visit our help center.
