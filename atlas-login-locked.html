Protecting
donor data takes vigilance.

Atlas locks any account after five consecutive failed sign-in attempts. We log the source IP, notify your diocesan admin, and hold the lockout for thirty minutes — long enough to stop most credential-stuffing attacks.

Brute-force protection. Failed attempts are tracked per account and per IP, with exponential back-off.
Audit logged. Every lockout writes to the immutable seven-year audit trail for compliance reviews.
Admin override. Diocesan admins can clear a lockout instantly from Users & Roles if it's a false positive.
