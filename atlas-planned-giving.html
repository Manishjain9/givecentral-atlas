IRS compliance rules — auto-applied by Atlas IRS Notice 2007-7
Donor must be age 70½ or older at time of distribution. Atlas flags donors under this threshold and holds the QCD coding for review.
Transfer must be direct from IRA custodian to the Diocese. Donor cannot receive the funds first. Atlas records the custodian name on every QCD gift.
Acknowledgement must state no goods or services were provided in exchange. The Atlas QCD template includes this language automatically.
Annual QCD limit: $105,000 per donor (2026, indexed). Atlas tracks cumulative QCDs per donor per calendar year and alerts when approaching the limit.
QCDs cannot be made to donor-advised funds. If a donor attempts a QCD to a DAF-held fund, Atlas flags for review before the gift is coded.
