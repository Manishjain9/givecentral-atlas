Our Story

The Catholic Community Foundation fosters faith-based stewardship for the charitable, educational, and spiritual needs of our diocese. We help donors continue the mission of Jesus to transform the world by connecting their support to ministries and services that lift up the Catholic community as well as the entire community. We are a non-profit 501(c)(3) with a Board of Directors that includes Catholic, religious and community leaders.
